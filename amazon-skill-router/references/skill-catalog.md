# Amazon Skill Catalog

This catalog lists the child skills available through `amazon-skill-router`. All entries are Amazon-only.

## amazon-review-analysis

- **Use for**: Amazon buyer-review analysis, review strengths and weaknesses, frequency and severity grouping, product-development insights, or a reusable review-analysis prompt.
- **Fuzzy calls**: `评论分析`, `调用评论分析 skill`, `亚马逊评论分析`, `买家评论分析`, `review analysis`.
- **Typical inputs**: pasted review tables or review files with `ASIN | 标题 | 内容`.
- **Output**: ASIN-separated conclusions, strengths, weaknesses, priority improvements, product and operations suggestions, and analysis limitations.
